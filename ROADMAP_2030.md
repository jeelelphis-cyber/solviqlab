# SolviqLab — Strategic Roadmap 2030

> Every technical decision must answer one question:
> **"Does this bring us closer to the company we want to be in 2030?"**

---

## Mission

Make intelligent decisions accessible to every person on Earth —
regardless of background, language, income, or location.

---

## Vision 2030

SolviqLab becomes the world's most trusted **AI Decision Platform** —
the infrastructure layer that powers intelligent, personalized guidance
across Health, Finance, Education, and Life decisions.

Not a calculator site. Not a chatbot. A platform.

---

## The Five Laws

1. **Intelligence over information.** We don't show data. We interpret it.
2. **Platform over product.** Every product makes the platform smarter.
3. **Trust over growth.** We never sacrifice user trust for metrics.
4. **Graph over session.** Every interaction builds a persistent user model.
5. **Openness over lock-in.** Developers can build on our platform.

---

## Phases

### Phase 1 — Foundation (2025–2026)
*Build the platform core. Prove the model works.*

**Products:** Health calculators, Mia coach, Alex coach
**Platform:** Member Platform, Event Bus, Intelligence Graph v1
**Business:** Trial → PRO conversion, first 1,000 paying users
**Goal:** Prove that AI + Graph creates measurably better outcomes than static calculators

---

### Phase 2 — Expansion (2026–2027)
*More verticals. More coaches. More languages.*

**Products:**
- Finance vertical (budget, debt, investment risk)
- Education vertical (learning style, cognitive assessment)
- Life vertical (stress, relationships, decision fatigue)

**Platform:**
- Recommendation Engine v1
- AI Context Engine (universal coach contract)
- Platform Dashboard v1 (CEO observability)
- Developer API v1 (public beta)

**Business:**
- 10,000 paying users
- First B2B partnerships (HR, wellness programs)
- API revenue begins

---

### Phase 3 — Intelligence (2027–2028)
*The Graph becomes the product.*

**Platform:**
- Intelligence Graph v2 (cross-vertical connections)
- Predictive recommendations (before user asks)
- Longitudinal tracking (6-month user journeys)
- Coach memory (remembers everything across sessions)

**Products:**
- Coach families: Health, Finance, Career, Mind, Life
- Personalized weekly plans generated from Graph
- Progress tracking and outcome measurement

**Business:**
- 100,000 users
- Enterprise tier (companies buy access for employees)
- First research partnerships (universities, health institutions)

---

### Phase 4 — Platform (2028–2029)
*Others build on SolviqLab.*

**Developer Platform:**
- Public API: Graph reads, event publishing, coach integration
- SDK: JavaScript, Python, mobile
- Marketplace: third-party coaches, calculators, assessments
- Webhooks: push user events to external systems

**Enterprise:**
- White-label platform (companies run their own SolviqLab instance)
- HIPAA / GDPR compliant data layer
- Custom coach training on enterprise data
- SSO, audit logs, admin dashboard

**Business:**
- $1M ARR
- Developer ecosystem: 500+ third-party products
- Enterprise contracts

---

### Phase 5 — Research & Intelligence (2029–2030)
*SolviqLab becomes a knowledge institution.*

**Research:**
- Anonymized population health/finance/decision insights
- Published research in partnership with universities
- AI models trained on consent-based platform data
- Innovation Lab: 6 researchers exploring next-decade problems

**Platform:**
- Intelligence Graph v3: societal-scale patterns
- Collective Intelligence: what works for people like you
- Outcome prediction: AI models that predict 6-month user outcomes

**Business:**
- $10M ARR
- Data licensing to research institutions
- IPO readiness or strategic acquisition

## AI Strategy

**2025–2026:** AI as coach (Mia, Alex — conversational guidance)
**2026–2027:** AI as analyst (reads your Graph, generates insights)
**2027–2028:** AI as planner (builds your 30/90/180-day plan)
**2028–2029:** AI as predictor (models your outcome before you decide)
**2029–2030:** AI as researcher (contributes to population-level knowledge)

**Model strategy:**
- Use best available model per task (not vendor-locked)
- DeepSeek for cost efficiency, Anthropic for reasoning depth
- Fine-tune on platform data when scale permits
- Never expose raw model to users — always through Platform contracts

Review quarterly. Promote to PROTOTYPE when evidence justifies.

---

## What We Will Never Do

- Sell user data to advertisers
- Build addictive engagement mechanics
- Launch a product we don't fully understand
- Optimize for session time over outcomes
- Scale a broken experience

---

## The Test

Before any major decision, ask:

1. Does this make users make better decisions?
2. Does this make the platform smarter for the next user?
3. Does this bring us closer to 2030?
4. Would we be proud of this in 10 years?

If the answer to any is no — stop and reconsider.
